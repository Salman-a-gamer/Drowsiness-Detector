# 60-second pitch

0–8s: “This is Wakey Wakey lil bro: Drowsiness Detector, a Windows app that runs entirely on your PC.”

8–17s: “Choose your webcam and calibrate with eyes open. We calculate eye aspect ratio locally.”

17–24s: “Normal blinks are ignored. Sustained closure triggers one scare, with a cooldown and open-eye rearming.”

24–31s: “The monster rushes forward with audio and glitches. Any key or mouse movement dismisses it; 15 seconds is the maximum.”

31–41s: “Every phrase is visible and editable. Preview speech before saving, or use a recording. Disable all phrases for a silent voice.”

41–50s: “Local window titles and real closure duration personalize lines. Customize images, music, and voices.”

50–60s: “No webcam video is saved or sent anywhere. The app works offline after model setup. The demo uses simulated readings so it captures no personal video.”
