# Dependencies and assets

- Piper: OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl, GPL-3.0. Voice models have their own model cards/licenses; models are downloaded, not committed to this repository.
- MediaPipe: Apache-2.0; official Face Landmarker model downloaded by setup.
- OpenCV: Apache-2.0. NumPy: BSD. PySide6/Qt: LGPL/GPL/commercial terms; retain Qt notices when distributing a binary.
- mss: MIT. PyInstaller and its bootloader have their documented licensing/exception terms.
- Creepster font: SIL Open Font License; full notice in assets/fonts/OFL.txt.
- assets/monster.svg is an original code-generated fallback illustration.
- User images and music remain local and are excluded from Git. The public demo uses the original fallback and generated audio, not the supplied FNAF soundtrack.

Dependency/model rights remain with their authors. Review model cards and distribution obligations before publishing binary releases. No third-party audio or personal recordings are uploaded by the app.
