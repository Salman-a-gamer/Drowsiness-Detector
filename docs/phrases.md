# Phrase catalog

All of these lines are visible in Scene studio. Unknown placeholders are skipped; disabling all entries produces no speech.

- Wakey wakey, lil bro.
- Rise and shine. I've been waiting.
- I can see you.
- Did you think I wasn't watching?
- Your eyes were closed for {seconds} seconds. I counted.
- It's {time}. You should be asleep. Why aren't you?
- Don't stop working. I like watching you work.
- I was right behind you.
- Sleep is where I live.
- Close your eyes again. I dare you.
- Why are you still in {app}? Look at me instead.
- You looked so peaceful. I almost didn't wake you.
- Five more minutes... and I'll be closer.
- Good morning. Did you miss me?
- Don't worry. I'll watch over you. Forever.
- {app} can't hide you from me.
- Keep staring at {app}. I'll stand behind you.
- I was inside {app} before you opened it.
- You opened {app}. You let me in.
- {name}, I know when you close your eyes.
- Don't turn around, {name}. You already know I'm here.
- {name}, this screen isn't the only thing watching you.

`{name}` is only supplied when the user enables personalization and provides a
name. Windows autofill is an editable account-name suggestion, not a guaranteed
real name. Missing names skip these lines.
